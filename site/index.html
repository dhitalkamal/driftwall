Good to know

Why does macOS warn me when I open it?

Driftwall is not notarized yet, which needs a paid Apple developer account. Right-click Driftwall in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. Or run this once:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Driftwall.app

Can it play on the Lock Screen?

No. macOS only allows Lock Screen video through its own wallpaper system, which Driftwall doesn’t use.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is MIT licensed with no paid tier, and the code is on GitHub.
