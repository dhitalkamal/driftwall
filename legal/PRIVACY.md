# Driftwall Privacy Policy

Draft. Have a lawyer review before commercial use. Placeholders in ALL CAPS.

Driftwall is a local macOS app. It is built to keep your data on your device.

- What it stores locally: your selected video file paths and app settings, in
  ~/Library/Application Support/Driftwall/, and your license key. These never leave your Mac
  except as described below.
- Video files: Driftwall reads the video files you choose to play them. It does not upload,
  copy, or transmit them.
- License activation: your license key is verified on-device using an embedded public key. No
  network call is required to verify a license.
- Updates: if auto-update is enabled, the app requests an update feed from UPDATE_FEED_HOST.
  That request includes standard information such as your IP address and app version.
- Purchases: payments are handled by our merchant of record, MERCHANT_NAME, under their
  privacy policy. We receive order and license details, not your full payment data.
- Analytics: NONE by default. If analytics are added later, this policy will be updated and
  the data described here.

You can remove all local data by deleting the app and the folder above.
