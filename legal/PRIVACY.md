# Driftwall Privacy

Driftwall is a local, open-source macOS app. It keeps your data on your device.

- **What it stores locally:** your selected video file paths and app settings, in
  `~/Library/Application Support/Driftwall/`. These never leave your Mac.
- **Video files:** Driftwall reads the video files you choose in order to play them. It does
  not upload, copy, or transmit them.
- **Network:** Driftwall makes no network requests. There is no account, license check,
  telemetry, or analytics.

You can remove all local data by deleting the app and the folder above.
